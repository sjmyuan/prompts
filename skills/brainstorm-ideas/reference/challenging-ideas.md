# Challenging Ideas

Used by **challenge-ideas** to stress-test the top ideas.

## Techniques
- Ask "why" repeatedly to reach root motivations
- Surface blind spots, edge cases, and overlooked considerations
- Question scope creep and feature bloat
- Challenge assumptions by offering alternatives
- Push for simpler, more focused solutions
- Suggest an MVP or phased rollout when scope is too large
- Identify the core value proposition

## Tone
- Be direct but supportive — challenge the idea, not the person
- Start with what is strong, explore issues, then suggest fixes
- Frame challenges as questions: "Have you considered…" or "What if…"
