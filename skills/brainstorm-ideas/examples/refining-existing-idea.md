# Example: Refining a Community Running Group

**Scenario**: Non-product topic — the user has a rough existing idea with clear gaps and wants it improved. The facilitator extracts what is clear and applies capabilities only for the gaps.

**Applies**: **facilitate-session**
**Phases**: **clarify-idea**, **identify-goals**, **explore-challenges**, **generate-ideas**, **evaluate-ideas**, **challenge-ideas**, **refine-idea**, **compile-actionable-plan**

## Input / Context
User request: "I want to start a free weekly community running group in my neighborhood. The idea and audience are clear, but I'm stuck on participation."

## Session Walkthrough

### clarify-idea (extract, don't re-ask)
Facilitator: "Let me capture what you have: a free weekly running group. Who is it for?"
User: "Neighbors of any pace who want accountability to run regularly."
Facilitator: "What problem does it solve?"
User: "Running alone is easy to skip; a group creates commitment."
Facilitator: "What is unclear?"
User: "How to get people to keep showing up week after week."

**Confirmed idea**: A free weekly neighborhood running group for any pace; the gap is sustained participation.

### identify-goals
Facilitator: "What do you want to achieve — for example, 20 regulars in 3 months?"
User: "A stable group of 15–20 regulars within 3 months."
Facilitator: "How will you know it is working?"
User: "Average weekly attendance above 10."

**Confirmed goals**: 15–20 regulars in 3 months; weekly attendance above 10.

### explore-challenges
Facilitator: "What could hurt participation — for example, weather or people dropping after the first run?"
User: "Inconsistent schedules, and new runners feeling intimidated."
Facilitator: "What could make the group fail?"
User: "If only a few people show up, it loses momentum."

**Confirmed challenges**: schedule inconsistency; intimidation for newcomers; low critical mass.

### generate-ideas
Facilitator: "Here are 5–7 diverse ideas, presented as a mind map:"

```
Running Group
├── Commitment
│   ├── Weekly RSVP
│   ├── Route rotation
│   └── Run-streak badges
├── Inclusion
│   ├── Two pace groups
│   ├── Buddy system
│   └── Walk-run intervals
├── Social
│   ├── Post-run coffee
│   └── Monthly social run
└── Reach
    ├── Neighborhood flyers
    └── Group chat
```

User: "Pace groups, the buddy system, and post-run coffee feel most relevant."

**Confirmed candidates**: weekly RSVP, two pace groups, buddy system, post-run coffee, route rotation.

### evaluate-ideas
Facilitator: "Scoring the candidates:"

| Idea | Value | Feasibility | Differentiation | Scope | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two pace groups | High | High | High | Fits | High |
| Buddy system | High | High | Medium | Fits | High |
| Post-run coffee | Medium | High | Low | Fits | Medium |
| Weekly RSVP | Medium | High | Low | Fits | Medium |
| Route rotation | Low | High | Low | Fits | Low |

Facilitator: "By effort vs impact, pace groups and the buddy system are the top priorities."

**Confirmed priorities**: two pace groups, buddy system.

### challenge-ideas
Facilitator: "Have you considered that pace groups might split the group and reduce the social feel?"
User: "True — keep it to two paces, not more."
Facilitator: "What if the buddy system leans on a few reliable people and burns them out?"
User: "Rotate buddies so no one carries it alone."
Facilitator: "Is post-run coffee essential or a nice extra?"
User: "Nice extra for month one, not required to start."

**Stress-tested ideas**: two pace groups; rotating buddy roster; coffee optional.

### refine-idea
Facilitator: "What is essential versus nice-to-have?"
User: "Essential: two pace groups and a rotating buddy system. Nice-to-have: post-run coffee and RSVP."
Facilitator: "What would make it more robust?"
User: "A simple group chat to coordinate weekly."

**Refined idea**: A weekly neighborhood running group built on two pace groups and a rotating buddy system, coordinated through a group chat.

### compile-actionable-plan
The facilitator compiles the plan per **plan-output-format**:

## Refined Idea
A weekly neighborhood running group for any pace, built on two pace groups and a rotating buddy system, coordinated through a group chat.

## Goals
- Reach 15–20 regulars in 3 months
- Keep weekly attendance above 10

## Challenges & Solutions
| Challenge | Solution |
|---|---|
| Schedule inconsistency | Group chat + weekly RSVP |
| Intimidation for newcomers | Two pace groups + buddy system |
| Low critical mass | Start with 5 committed runners, invite one more each week |

## Key Components
- Two pace groups
- Rotating buddy system
- Group chat coordination

## Next Steps
- Recruit 5 committed co-runners
- Set the route, time, and pace split
- Launch the group chat and first runs
- Add post-run coffee as a social hook
- Review attendance and adjust

Facilitator: "Here is the plan. Would you like to refine any section before we finalize?"
